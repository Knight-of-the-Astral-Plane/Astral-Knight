Knights of the Astral Plane

A tiny, strange game about an aspiring knight skipping stones across the astral plane.

Offline. No ads. No accounts. No subscriptions.

Privacy Policy

Knights of the Astral Plane does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal or device data.

The game works entirely offline and does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, user accounts, cloud services, or third-party advertising or analytics services.

Game progress and settings are stored locally on the user's device only. The developer does not receive or retain this data and cannot restore or delete it remotely. Users can remove locally stored game data by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.
